Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Double Dynamics Tech Corp. ("Double Dynamics", "we"), of North Vancouver, British Columbia, builds and operates software services. This policy applies to all of our services and to this website. It explains what personal information we handle, why, how long we keep it, and what choices you have.

Our privacy officer is responsible for this policy. Questions and requests go to info@doubledynamics.ca.

Information we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and Google's identifier for your account. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google data.
  • Content you submit. The files and other content you give a service, and what the service produces from them. Content can contain personal information about you and other people.
  • Usage and billing records. Your plan, what you have used, your purchases and your payment status.
  • Payment provider identifiers. The references our payment provider gives us for your customer record and purchases. Your card details are entered on the payment provider's pages and never reach our servers.
  • Sign-in session data. What we need to keep you signed in securely.
  • Technical logs. Our servers record technical data such as IP addresses and requests.

If a service lets you try it without an account, we collect only the content you submit and technical logs.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, provide the service you asked for, handle billing, answer support requests, keep our services secure and meet our legal obligations.

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or use your information for advertising.
  • We never use your content to train models.
  • We look at your content only when you ask us to investigate a problem, or when the law requires it.

Information from Google

We use your Google email address and account identifier only to sign you in and to identify your account. We do not transfer this information to anyone except our hosting provider, which stores it for us, and we do not use it for advertising or to train models.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the providers that run our services, and only as needed for their part of it:

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our services and stores your account data and content.
  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe sells our paid plans as merchant of record, through its Link service, and handles the information you give at checkout under its own privacy policy.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • GitHub hosts this website and may log visitors' technical data.

We use no analytics, advertising, error tracking or support tools. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Your account data and content are stored and processed in the United States. Our providers may also process information in other countries. Information held outside Canada is subject to the laws of those countries, and their courts and authorities may be able to access it.

How long we keep it

  • Content you submit, and what a service produces from it, is deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Account details and usage records are kept while your account is open.
  • Billing records are kept for 6 years, as Canadian tax law requires.
  • Technical logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Backups may hold deleted information for up to 35 days before it is overwritten.

Cookies

Our services use only the cookies needed for sign-in and security. We use no analytics or advertising cookies. Google and Stripe set their own cookies on their own pages.

Security

We protect personal information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit, private storage and limited access. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw your consent, or ask us to delete your account. Write to info@doubledynamics.ca from the email address on your account. We answer within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes all of your information except billing records, which we must keep for 6 years. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide a service to you.

You can also remove our access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Children

Our services are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will also notify you by email or in the service before it takes effect.
